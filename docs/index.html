Questions

My timer didn't ring

Spaghetti Timer rings through iOS alarms, so it needs permission to schedule them. If you didn't allow alarms the first time you started a timer, open the Settings app, find Spaghetti Timer and turn alarms on.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

The free version lets you pin up to 3 presets in total and try auto-restart and End time once each. Spaghetti Timer Pro is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited pinned presets, auto-restart, End time timers and the To next hour tile.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Pro usually comes back on its own; if it doesn't, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Can my family use Pro?

Yes. Pro supports Family Sharing, so members of your Family Sharing group get it too.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Spaghetti Timer. Tap a preset on the widget to start it. Running timers also appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
