Spaghetti Timer Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Spaghetti Timer has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or contacts. Your timers stay on your iPhone. The app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see which features people use. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, we don't use your device's advertising identifier, and we don't sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your presets, running timers and settings. They're stored on your iPhone and shared only with Spaghetti Timer's own widgets and Live Activities.
  • How many free pins and free tries you've used. This is stored in your iPhone's Keychain so it survives a reinstall. It isn't synced or sent anywhere.

What the app sends to Google Analytics for Firebase

Spaghetti Timer uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google, to count how features are used. It sends:

  • Usage events: when a timer starts, pauses, resumes, finishes, repeats or is cancelled; when a preset is created, pinned or deleted; and when the Pro screen is shown or Pro is bought or restored. Each event carries details such as the timer's length, whether it restarts automatically, and where the action came from (the app, a widget, a Live Activity or an alarm). The built-in presets are reported by name. Names you type for your own presets never leave your iPhone; they're reported only as “custom”.
  • Data Google Analytics collects automatically: a random ID for this installation of the app, app opens and sessions, the app and iOS version, the device model, your language, an approximate location (country or region) that Google works out from your IP address, and in-app purchase events reported by the App Store (which product and its price).
  • Firebase installation data: a random installation ID and basic technical information Firebase needs to work.

We use this only to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It isn't linked to your identity, isn't used for advertising and isn't shared with data brokers. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Event data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

Purchases

Spaghetti Timer Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app asks the App Store whether you own Pro so it can unlock it.

Permissions

Spaghetti Timer asks to schedule alarms so your timers can ring even when the app is closed or your iPhone is locked. It's used for nothing else.

Children

Spaghetti Timer isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes your presets and timers from your iPhone. Because the analytics data isn't tied to your identity, we usually can't find the data that belongs to one person, but if you have a question or a request about your data, write to us and we'll help where we can.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
